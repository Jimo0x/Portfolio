    How I work

    1. 01

      Scoping

      We set the goal, scope, and timeline together. For a penetration test, nothing starts without written authorization and a precise list of what can be tested.

    2. 02

      Analysis or build

      Recon and testing for an audit, step-by-step development for an app. You get regular progress updates, not three weeks of silence.

    3. 03

      Report

      Every finding comes with its severity, the proof behind it, and the proposed fix, ranked from most to least urgent. For development: the code, its documentation, and the deployment.

    4. 04

      Follow-up

      I verify the fixes actually close the reported issues, or I support the first weeks of the app in production.
